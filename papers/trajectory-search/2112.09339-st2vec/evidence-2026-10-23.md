# Evidence — ST2Vec

Version: arXiv:2112.09339v2; formal paper DOI 10.1145/3534678.3539375, KDD 2022. Checked: 2026-10-23 via local PDF, alphaXiv page extraction, and Crossref.

- PDF p.1: learns complete-trajectory embeddings that preserve fine-grained spatial and temporal similarity; top-k retrieval and clustering are downstream case studies.
- PDF §II: a road-network trajectory is a synchronized sequence of road vertices and timestamps.
- PDF §II-B: the learning target is a weighted linear combination of a spatial trajectory measure and a temporal trajectory measure.
- The model uses location/time, not POI categories or text semantics, and does not return a matching subtrajectory interval.
- Formal publication title is `Spatio-Temporal Trajectory Similarity Learning in Road Networks`; the arXiv metadata also exposes the alternate title supplied by the user.

Classification: direct time-aware whole-trajectory similarity prior; it removes any claim that timestamps have not been integrated into trajectory similarity.
