# Evidence — Spatio-Temporal Trajectory Similarity Measures survey

Version: arXiv:2303.05012v2; formal version DOI 10.1109/TKDE.2023.3323535, TKDE 36(5), 2024. Checked: 2026-10-23 via local PDF, alphaXiv page extraction, IEEE, and Crossref.

- PDF pp.1–3: surveys 25 trajectory measures using three axes: learning/non-learning, free-space/road-network, and standalone/distributed.
- It evaluates effectiveness, robustness, efficiency, and scalability under length, shape, noise, sampling, and cardinality shifts.
- The core data model is GPS points `(latitude,longitude[,timestamp])`; REPOSE and ST2Vec appear in its taxonomy.
- The survey mentions POI-based models such as GTS, but semantic POI/text similarity is not a primary taxonomy axis and the paper is not a semantic-episode retrieval method.

Classification: background taxonomy and benchmark source, not a direct query-method baseline. It should guide distance and evaluation choices without being cited as evidence that semantic trajectory retrieval is solved.
